# --- Leave Policy ---
Q: How many weeks of paid maternity leave are employees entitled to?
A: 26 weeks for the first or second child, as per the Maternity Benefit Act 2017.
SRC: Leave_Policy_-_2026_1775712658732.pdf

Q: How many days of bereavement leave does an employee get?
A: 3 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member.
SRC: Leave_Policy_-_2026_1775712658732.pdf

Q: What is the leave year period defined in the Leave Policy?
A: The year commences on the 1st of January and ends on the 31st of December (calendar year).
SRC: Leave_Policy_-_2026_1775712658732.pdf

Q: Are Saturdays and Sundays counted as part of leave?
A: No. Saturdays, Sundays, or holidays that fall at the beginning, in between, or at the end of leave are not counted, except in the case of maternity leave.
SRC: Leave_Policy_-_2026_1775712658732.pdf
